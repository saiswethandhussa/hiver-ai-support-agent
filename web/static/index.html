Top 5 Failure Modes Analysis & Diagnostic Hypotheses

#1 Intent Boundary Blurring (Return vs Damaged vs Exchange)

Example: "@AmazonHelp The zipper on this jacket broke the very first time I zipped it up. Can I exchange for the same size?"

Hypothesis: Customer simultaneously mentions physical defect ("zipper broke") and post-purchase workflow ("exchange"). Single-label classification splits probability between `DAMAGED_OR_WRONG_ITEM` and `RETURN_AND_REFUND`.

Mitigation: Multi-label intent tagging with hierarchical precedence (Physical Damage > Self-Service Exchange).

#2 Sarcasm & Passive-Aggressive Tone Inversion

Example: "Huge thanks @AmazonHelp for throwing my laptop over the fence so it could swim in the puddle! Incredible service!"

Hypothesis: High positive lexical tokens ("thanks", "incredible service") falsely satisfy positive sentiment priors and miss the underlying property destruction.

Mitigation: Semantic contradiction detector between customer sentiment tokens and negative object predicates ("threw laptop", "puddle").

#3 Multi-Turn Context Truncation

Example: "I was promised a promotional credit refund by your chat agent yesterday and it never showed up in my account @AmazonHelp"

Hypothesis: Agent has access only to single-turn Twitter text and lacks the historical chat transcript/CRM ID, forcing an escalation to human agent.

Mitigation: Deep CRM integration to resolve external conversation thread IDs before initiating triage.

#4 Ambiguous / Minimal Input Tokens

Example: "@AmazonHelp ." or "@AmazonHelp ???"

Hypothesis: Degenerate inputs generate uniform low-entropy probability distributions across all 7 classes.

Mitigation: Confidence guardrail directly routes queries with length < 5 to `LOW_CONFIDENCE_OR_AMBIGUITY` clarification response.

#5 Policy Edge Cases (Accidental Minor In-App Purchases)

Example: "My 6-year-old child bought $300 of Roblox coins on my tablet without permission @AmazonHelp"

Hypothesis: Digital items are normally strictly non-refundable under automated rules, but parental accidental exception requires manual agent discretion.

Mitigation: Dedicated Parental Exception policy branch inside the escalation triage rule tree.

Decision Log: 12 Non-Obvious Architectural Decisions

01

Brand Choice: @AmazonHelp

Selected over airlines or telcos due to clear bifurcated ground truth: high-volume self-service FAQ links vs strict high-stakes escalations (carrier theft, compromised credentials).

02

7-Intent Taxonomy

Restricted to 7 mutually exclusive core intents derived from real data rather than Banking77's 77 fragmented classes, maximizing classification confidence and production reliability.

03

Recall-Oriented Escalation

Optimized escalation for high recall (93.3%) over raw precision, prioritizing safety and preventing high-risk customer churn or security leaks.

04

Margin-Calibrated Confidence

Used probability margin between top-1 and top-2 classes rather than raw softmax, protecting against multi-class uncertainty in 7-way classification.

05

Grounding over Free-Form LLM Generation

Constrained replies to verified historical brand resolution patterns to eliminate hallucinations of fake order tracking numbers or unauthorized refund promises.

06

Strict 280-Character Twitter Limit

Enforced Twitter-length conciseness constraint in prompt and scoring rubric to match real-world Twitter customer service operating dynamics.

07

5-Tier Stratified Golden Set

Engineered test splits across Standard, Noisy/Typos, High-Anger/Churn, Ambiguous/Edge-Case, and Multi-Turn tiers to prevent benchmark saturation.

08

Zero-Leakage Partitioning

Strictly isolated the 200 Golden Evaluation samples from the 500-pair historical knowledge base memory to guarantee genuine generalization measurement.

09

Tri-Model Baseline Benchmarking

Benchmarked against both a Trivial Baseline (Majority class + canned reply) and a Simple Baseline (Naive Bayes + Naive Rule) to prove incremental value.

10

Human-Judge Agreement Calibration

Validated LLM Judge with 50 human-annotated double-scored samples, calculating Cohen's Kappa, Pearson r, and Adjacent Agreement (88.8%).

11

Offline Deterministic Fallback

Equipped the pipeline with high-speed local inference ensuring sub-15 minute reproducibility and zero external API dependencies for evaluation.

12

Explicit Stated Escalation Reason

Mandated a structured reason category (`SECURITY_OR_PII_RISK`, `POLICY_EXCEPTION_OR_INVESTIGATION`, etc.) with human-readable rationale for explainable triage.
